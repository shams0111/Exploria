02 Our story

A passion project, rooted in the place we call home.

Exploria began in 2016 as a passion project, rooted in our love for travel and a deep connection to the place we call home.

We are natives of the beautiful Hunza and Barjungle, some of Pakistan’s most celebrated travel destinations, where tourism has been an integral part of community life for generations. Here, welcoming travellers is more than an economic activity; it is deeply woven into the region’s cultural heritage, traditions and way of life.

Growing up in this environment gave us a natural appreciation for the beauty of our homeland and the importance of sharing it with others. We witnessed firsthand how travel brings people, cultures and communities together, creating connections that extend far beyond the journey itself.

Inspired by this heritage and driven by a passion to showcase Pakistan to the world, we set out to create something of our own.

That passion became Exploria.
